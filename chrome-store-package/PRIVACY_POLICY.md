# Privacy Policy for TempMail Pro

**Last updated: January 2025**

## Overview

TempMail Pro is a Chrome extension that helps users create and manage temporary email addresses from multiple providers. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data Collection and Usage

### What We Collect
- **Temporary Email Addresses**: The extension stores temporary email addresses you create locally in your browser
- **Email Messages**: Messages received at temporary addresses are fetched and displayed locally
- **User Preferences**: Service provider selection, refresh intervals, and other settings are stored locally

### What We Don't Collect
- **Personal Information**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information
- **Permanent Email Addresses**: We do not access your personal email accounts
- **Browsing History**: We do not track your browsing activity
- **User Analytics**: We do not collect usage analytics or tracking data

## Data Storage

### Local Storage Only
- All data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API
- No data is transmitted to our servers or any third-party services (except the temporary email providers)
- Data is automatically cleared when temporary emails expire

### Third-Party Services
This extension connects to the following temporary email service providers:
- Guerrilla Mail (guerrillamail.com)
- Mail.gw (mail.gw)
- DropMail.me (dropmail.me)
- Mail.tm (mail.tm)
- TempMail.lol (tempmail.lol)

Each service has its own privacy policy and data handling practices. We recommend reviewing their policies if you have concerns about data handling.

## Permissions

The extension requests the following permissions:
- **Storage**: To save your temporary email addresses and preferences locally
- **Alarms**: To schedule automatic inbox refreshes
- **Notifications**: To alert you of new messages
- **Clipboard Write**: To copy email addresses to your clipboard
- **Host Permissions**: To communicate with temporary email service APIs

## Data Security

- All communication with email services uses HTTPS encryption
- Data is stored using Chrome's secure storage APIs
- No sensitive data is transmitted outside of the necessary API calls to email providers

## Your Rights

You can:
- Clear all stored data by removing the extension
- Disable notifications in Chrome settings
- Choose which email service providers to use
- Control automatic refresh intervals

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.
